Deadlines: an Ice is due by the start of the 3rd quarter of the following week's Monday night game, counted as 9:45 PM Eastern. The first missed deadline costs the team 2 points, and the penalty doubles for each week the Ice stays undone (−2, then −4, −8 and so on). Only the latest penalty counts, and doing the Ice late freezes it.
